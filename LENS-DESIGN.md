# A11yLens Design Direction

A11yLens belongs to the LENS family alongside LinkLens and ImageLens. Its blue magnifying lens contains **A11y** lettering. Preserve the current [logo](brand/a11ylens-mark.svg), [semantic tokens](lens-tokens.css), and bundled Geist font.

- Prioritize issue triage, impact, affected elements, and remediation evidence.
- Preserve system/light/dark appearance, visible focus, native controls, and modal focus restoration.
- Keep pass, violation, needs-review, and unverified coverage distinct, with text labels as well as color.
- Support narrow viewports and enlarged text; contain wide evidence in scrollable regions.
- Keep scanner and storage contracts intact. Automated results are not conformance claims.
- Use brief interaction feedback and respect reduced motion.

See [design validation](DESIGN-VALIDATION.md) for recorded checks and limitations.
